Poker Odds Calculator

This Privacy Policy explains how Poker Odds Calculator handles user information.

Information Collection

Poker Odds Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app operates entirely on your device and does not require account registration, login, or cloud storage.

Data Usage

All poker calculations and simulations are processed locally on your device. No gameplay data or card selections are transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Poker Odds Calculator does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or stored, your privacy remains protected while using the app.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact
